What is CIRRUS?

CIRRUS is a Cloud Native platform for scientific research. Your code is packaged into containers, Kubernetes runs them at scale, and your git repository defines exactly what gets deployed. Push a change, and CIRRUS updates your workload to match.

Why Researchers Choose CIRRUS

Focus on science, not servers

Always Running

Your workload stays up around the clock. If a server fails or goes down for maintenance, CIRRUS moves your workload and keeps it running.

Handled for You

Certificates, web addresses, and the servers underneath are maintained by the CIRRUS team, so you aren't stuck being a system administrator.

Reproducible by Design

Your workload is defined in code and kept in git. You can roll back a change, collaborators can review it, and anyone can rebuild it the same way.

Scales With You

Run more than one copy of your workload with traffic balanced between them, and add resources as your project grows.

No Allocations

CIRRUS currently has no allocations associated with its use, and the team's time helping you doesn't come out of your project budget.

We Help You Onboard

You don't need to learn Kubernetes first. We'll meet with you, help package and deploy your code, and you can go as deep as you want over time.

How It Works

From first conversation to running on CIRRUS, with our team alongside you

Reach Out

Tell us what you want to run. We'll set up a meeting to talk through your project and the next steps.

Get Your Space

We create a namespace for your project: your own area of the cluster for you and your team, a bit like an HPC allocation.

Package and Deploy

We work with you to get your code into git, package it into a container, and define how it runs on CIRRUS.

Update Through Git

Once connected, your git repository drives everything. Push a change and CIRRUS updates your workload to match.
